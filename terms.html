ToolCoreX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  •  App: ToolCoreX (com.toolcorex.app)  •  Developer: ALTAF AD
In short: ToolCoreX has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your files are processed on your phone and are not uploaded. The camera is used only for scanning QR codes and barcodes, and nothing from the camera is saved. All tools work offline, and the app sends nothing about you to any server (see section 5).

1. Who we are

ToolCoreX is an Android app with PDF, image, audio, video, text, SEO, converter and calculator tools. This app was created with the help of AI. This policy explains what the app does with information. If you have questions, write to aadsupport@gmail.com.

2. No account, no ads, no tracking

  • You do not need to create an account or sign in.
  • The app shows no advertisements and contains no advertising SDKs.
  • The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or device identifiers.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, because we do not collect it.

3. Your files

When you open a file in a tool (for example a PDF, photo, audio or video file), it is read and processed on your phone by code that is bundled inside the app. The file is not uploaded to us or to any server. Results are saved only where you choose (for example your Downloads folder) or shared through the Android share sheet when you tap Share. Files shared into the app from other apps are handled the same way.

4. Camera

The camera permission is used only by the QR code and barcode scanning tools, to show a live preview and read the code. Camera images are analysed on the phone, are not saved and are not sent anywhere. You can also scan from a saved image instead, and you can deny or revoke the camera permission in Android settings; the other tools keep working.

5. Internet connections

All tools work fully offline. The app does not send your files, your inputs or any information about you to us or to any other server.

The only things that can touch the internet are ones you choose to do yourself:

  • HTML Viewer: it shows the HTML you paste in an isolated frame. If your HTML refers to images, scripts or fonts on other websites, those are loaded from those websites, which can see your IP address and handle it under their own privacy policies.
  • Links you tap: for example opening a map for coordinates found in a QR code or photo, “Rate us” (Google Play), or a link on a policy page. These open in your browser or another app.

The app’s fonts, scripts and text-recognition (OCR) data are bundled inside the app. It does not download them from third-party servers.

6. Information stored on your device

The app keeps a few small items in its private storage on your phone, only so that it can work the way you expect: your settings (theme, accent colour, language), favourite tools, recently used tools and usage counts, the cities you add to the World Clock, and whether the welcome walkthrough was shown. This information never leaves your phone. You can clear it in Settings → Data or by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it in Android settings.

7. Android permissions

  • Internet — only for the things described in section 5 (pasted HTML that loads outside content, and links you open).
  • Camera — for QR/barcode scanning (section 4). Optional.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) — to save the files you create into your Downloads folder. Newer Android versions do not need this permission.

8. Third-party code

ToolCoreX includes open-source libraries (for example for reading and writing PDFs, ZIP files, MP3 audio and QR codes). They run inside the app on your phone and do not send data anywhere.

9. Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Your choices

  • You can turn off internet access for the app in Android settings; all tools keep working.
  • Revoke the camera permission at any time in Android settings.
  • Delete all locally stored information by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app.

11. Not affiliated

ToolCoreX is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Google, PayPal, X or any other company or brand mentioned in the app. All names belong to their respective owners.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app works with information, we will update this page and the “Last updated” date. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
